Your personal data in Lighter

Lighter is a private, single-owner food and weight tracker. This page describes this version’s actual data flow; it is not a claim of regulatory certification.

What is stored

Your profile, goals, food entries, weights, water, hunger check-ins, activity and progress photos are stored on the server hosting your personal app. Your hosting provider operates that infrastructure. App-password verification uses a derived password hash. Fitbit tokens and saved connection keys are encrypted with the server’s separate encryption key. Health records and images are not individually encrypted by the application.

Fitbit connection

Only after you authorise Fitbit, the server can request your activity, weight, sleep, heart-rate and nutrition data within the permissions you grant. Daily syncing happens on request and, while the app is open and your personal journey is set up, periodically. This app does not import every historical or intraday record. Disconnecting Fitbit removes this app’s saved Fitbit tokens and latest raw sync, but does not remove diary entries or weight records already imported. You can also revoke permission from your Fitbit account settings.

AI is optional

A selected meal image is sent to OpenAI only when you choose to estimate it. Favourite food names, remaining budget and hunger level are sent when you request personalised snack suggestions. Body-progress photos are not sent to AI. OpenAI’s own API data-use and retention policies apply to those requests. Manual logging works without AI. Lighter does not sell your records or add advertising analytics.

Your browser and sign-in

The hosted private app uses an HttpOnly session cookie, expiring after seven days. Sign out on shared devices. Hosted health records and photos are not placed in the service worker’s offline cache or the app’s browser-storage backup; only public interface files are cached. Local development mode may store sample/manual records in browser storage.

Removing data

You can remove meals, weigh-ins and progress images in the app; deleting an image removes its server file. The app owner controls the hosting account and its storage. A complete copy or removal of the server database, backups and remaining records currently requires the owner’s hosting tools; there is not yet a complete account-export/deletion button. A persistent disk is not a backup by itself.

Personal use

This version has one owner, not separate accounts for other people. Do not invite others to submit health information. The app owner is responsible for their host and provider accounts and for reviewing this summary before connecting them.
