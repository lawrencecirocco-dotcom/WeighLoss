Using your private Lighter app

This is a personal, single-owner development app for adults. It is not a medical service or a public multi-user product.

Estimates, not guarantees

Energy budgets, photo nutrition, exercise credits, hydration reminders and projected weight dates are estimates. A photo cannot establish exact portions or hidden ingredients. Always review food estimates before confirming. No weight outcome or provider availability is guaranteed. Plans for pregnancy, breastfeeding, medical conditions or eating-disorder recovery should be agreed with a qualified clinician; this app is not intended for minors.

Your connected accounts

You decide whether to authorise Fitbit or use paid AI services. Your provider agreements, account eligibility and usage charges remain yours. A ChatGPT subscription does not include OpenAI API usage. Review your hosting and API prices and controls before activating them. You can keep manual logging without AI.

Storage and access

Protect your app password, phone, hosting account and connection keys. Arrange suitable backups. Do not share this owner account with other users or upload someone else’s private photos. The app depends on an internet connection for private records; it does not provide closed-phone reminders in this version.

Read how your personal data is handled before connecting services. These pages describe a personal test deployment and are not a substitute for legal or clinical advice if the project later becomes a public product.
